Why this matters now

Agents are becoming employees without managers.

The old observability stack can tell you latency, errors, and uptime. It cannot explain why an autonomous agent opened a tool, used a memory, skipped an approval, or gave a customer the wrong answer with total confidence.

01 / Silent failure

HTTP 200 does not mean the agent was right.

Most incidents look successful at the API layer. The damage happens in the decision chain.

02 / Missing evidence

Compliance cannot audit a black box.

Legal, security, and risk teams need durable proof of inputs, outputs, approvals, and model behavior.

03 / Manual investigation

Debugging agents is still detective work.

Teams stitch logs, chat history, traces, tool records, and screenshots together after the incident.

What AgentLens does

One audit layer across every agent run.

AgentLens sits beside your agent framework and creates a tamper-resistant record of the full workflow. It is built for engineers who debug incidents and leaders who need defensible answers.
